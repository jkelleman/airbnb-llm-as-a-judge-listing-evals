The stack

Each rule goes to the cheapest layer that can decide it the same way twice.

LAYER 1

Code checks

Anything countable or exactly matchable: length, punctuation, emoji, clichés, internal terms, waterfront words. Free, exact and never miscounts.

LAYER 2

Three virtual judges

Faithfulness claim by claim, salience against the home's standouts, and voice against the principles. One dimension per judge, binary, with typed errors.

LAYER 3

Blind human labels

Ground truth and the final call on what good means. Judges are calibrated against these labels, and rater agreement sets the ceiling no judge can beat.
